# UNIDAYS
UNIDAYS is a Flutter-based mobile application designed to bridge students and merchants by providing verified students with exclusive discounts and offers. The platform simplifies how students discover savings while giving merchants a dedicated space to promote their deals

## Project Overview
  - The application focuses on creating a trusted discount ecosystem where:

  - Students can register, verify their status, and access exclusive offers.

  - Merchants can publish, update, and manage discount campaigns in real time.

  - Discounts are centralized in one mobile-friendly platform for easy access.

**Core Features**
  - Secure student registration and account management
  - Merchant dashboard for creating and managing discount offers
  - Organized listing of discounts by category or merchant
  - Scalable Flutter architecture suitable for future expansion
